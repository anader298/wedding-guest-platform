The messages you paste in WhatsApp

Write the message once here. When you tap COPY INVITE on a family, the page fills in two things for you:
{name} becomes the family's name, like "The Salem Family".
{link} becomes that family's own invitation link. {link} must stay in the message, or they cannot open their invitation.

The links

Every family gets its own link, made automatically. You never type them: COPY INVITE puts the right one in the message.
